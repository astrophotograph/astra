“Your data should always be yours.”

Astra is a desktop observation log first and a gallery second. Sessions, equipment, and images live on your machine — publishing is something you choose, collection by collection, and can take back just as easily.

The instrument

Built around the log, not the likes

Observe

An honest observation log

Every session catalogued — targets, conditions, integration, notes. FITS metadata read straight from your files.

Equip

Your gear, remembered

Telescopes, mounts, cameras, filters. Seestar owners get presets out of the box — your images arrive already understood.

Publish

One-click galleries

Pick a collection, publish. Thumbnails, lightbox, object data — generated for you, straight from the desktop app.

Live

Auto-refreshing by default

Published galleries poll for new images every thirty seconds. Share the link at dusk; it's still growing at dawn.

Yours

Your data stays yours

Run the whole service on your own hardware if you like. No lock-in, no harvesting — the log lives with you.

Open

Free software, all the way down

Desktop app, gallery service, viewer — every line is AGPL v3. Read it, change it, run your own.
